# 🚴 Bike Sales Analysis (Excel Project)

## 📌 Overview
This project analyzes customer data to understand factors that influence bike purchases. The goal is to identify patterns in customer demographics and behavior to support better business decisions.

-<a href="https://github.com/Zeeshankhan-10/Excel-Projects-Hub/blob/main/Excel%20Project%20-%201.xlsx">Dataset View</a>

---

## 🎯 Objective
- Analyze customer data to identify trends in bike purchases  
- Understand how factors like income, age, and occupation impact buying behavior  
- Create insights that can help improve marketing and sales strategies  

---

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel  
- Data Cleaning  
- Pivot Tables  
- Data Visualization (Charts & Dashboards)

---

## 📊 Dataset
The dataset includes customer information such as:
- Age  
- Income  
- Gender  
- Marital Status  
- Education  
- Occupation  
- Region  
- Commute Distance  
- Bike Purchase Status  

---

## 🔄 Process
- Cleaned and organized raw data in Excel  
- Created pivot tables to analyze key trends  
- Built visualizations to compare customer segments  
- Identified relationships between demographics and bike purchases  

---

## 📈 Key Insights
- Higher income customers were more likely to purchase bikes  
- Commute distance had a strong impact on buying decisions  
- Certain age groups showed higher purchase rates  
- Occupation and education level influenced purchasing behavior  

---

## ✅ Conclusion
This analysis highlights key customer segments that are more likely to purchase bikes. These insights can help businesses target the right audience and improve sales strategies.
